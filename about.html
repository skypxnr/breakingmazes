About

Conversations that unmake the maze.

Breaking Mazes is a leadership dialogue series built on a simple conviction: that students deserve direct access to the people who have shaped institutions — not summaries of them.

The Curator

Prof. (Dr.) Peer G. N. Suhail

Founder & Curator, Breaking Mazes

Prof. Suhail has spent his career at the intersection of education, leadership and public life. Breaking Mazes is his attempt to build an archive of first-person insight — a resource that outlasts any single cohort of students.

He curates each session personally, treating the conversation as a discipline rather than a broadcast.

The Format

Three Commitments
01

Dialogue, not interview

Every session is a conversation with a shape — not a Q&A. The questions are as carefully chosen as the speaker.

02

Students in the room

Students are participants, not audiences. Their questions are recorded and archived as part of the session.

03

Built as an archive

Each session generates layers — video, clips, ideas, transcript, publication — designed to outlast the moment.
